# Product

## Register

product

## Users

UI/UX designers, frontend developers, content creators, and startup/product teams in Indonesia who need 3D isometric icon assets quickly and don't have the skill or time to design them from scratch. They land on the site to solve a concrete task (get icons for a UI, a deck, a thumbnail, a brand kit) and want to get from prompt to downloadable PNG with minimal friction.

## Product Purpose

Cubicon is an AI-powered 3D isometric icon generator. A user types a text prompt, the app generates an icon, and the user downloads it as a PNG. Monetized through a pay-per-use credit system (no subscriptions). Success looks like reaching break-even within 3 months at roughly 35% margin per transaction, driven by free-tier signups converting to paid credit packages.

## Brand Personality

Bold, energetic, confident. Expressed today through an existing neo-brutalist system: thick dark borders, offset drop shadows, a golden-yellow-on-deep-navy palette, and Syne/Epilogue typography. The tone is assertive and a little loud on purpose, not restrained.

## Anti-references

Generic flat, minimal SaaS aesthetics (Notion/Linear-style neutral cards, thin borders, soft shadows). That direction would erase the neo-brutalism identity that currently differentiates Cubicon and make it read as an interchangeable AI-tool template.

## Design Principles

- **Bold over safe** — lean into the existing neo-brutalist language (thick borders, hard shadows, saturated yellow) rather than softening it toward generic SaaS flatness.
- **Function drives form** — this is a product register: every visual decision should serve clarity and usability of the tool, not decoration for its own sake.
- **Show the craft, not stock imagery** — the product's output is literally generated 3D icons; real generated icons should carry visual weight over illustration or stock photography.
- **Credit transparency** — always surface cost before a paid action; trust matters more than conversion tricks in a pay-per-use model.
- **Mobile-first reliability** — the target market skews mobile-heavy; every page and element must degrade gracefully down to small screens without overlap, clipping, or illegible text.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA as the baseline: body text ≥4.5:1 contrast, large/bold text ≥3:1, touch targets sized appropriately on mobile. No additional accommodations requested beyond standard AA practice.
